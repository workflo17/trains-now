Click a train to see where it is going. Every dot is a real train, placed at the station it is at or on the track between the last station and the next one.

Positions come from the MTA's public realtime feed, refreshed every 25 seconds. The feed reports the station a train is at or heading for, not a coordinate, so a train in motion is drawn along the line between the two. Each route is drawn from one representative shape in the published schedule, so a branch a train rarely takes may be missing from the line even when the train is on it.
